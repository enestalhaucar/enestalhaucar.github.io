Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 20, 2026

Alarmly ("we," "us," or "our") operates the Alarmly mobile application (the "App"). This Privacy Policy explains what information the App uses, why, and who processes it on our behalf. The App has no accounts and no sign-in.

1. Information We Collect

  • No account: The App does not create an account, ask for a login, or collect your name, email address or phone number. A display name you may type in Settings stays on your device.
  • Anonymous identifiers: To manage your subscription and to understand how the App is used, the App uses random identifiers that are not linked to your identity: a purchase identifier generated by RevenueCat, and analytics identifiers generated by Google Firebase and Amplitude.
  • Usage data: How you use the App: alarm settings, wake-up challenges started and completed, streak progress, screens viewed, paywall views and purchase events, and the optional answers you give during onboarding (such as your age range, gender and morning habits).
  • Interaction recordings: With Microsoft Clarity, the App records how you move through its screens, such as taps, swipes, scrolls and the screens you visit, so we can see where the design is confusing. Your camera, photos, name and signature are masked and never recorded, and text you type is masked.
  • Purchases: Subscriptions and in-app purchases are processed by Apple. RevenueCat receives the purchase records Apple provides, so the App knows whether you have Alarmly Pro. We never see your payment details.
  • Device and diagnostic information: Device model, iOS version, app version, language, and crash reports, used to keep the App stable.

Photos you take for the photo challenge are analysed on your device and are never stored or uploaded. Voice recordings you make for alarm sounds stay on your device. Alarms, streaks and settings are stored locally on your device.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To provide the App: alarms through Apple's AlarmKit and notifications, wake-up challenges, streaks and statistics, and relaxation sounds.
  • To unlock Alarmly Pro when you subscribe, and to restore it on another device.
  • To fix crashes and improve the App through aggregate usage analytics.
  • To answer you when you contact us.

We do not use your information for advertising and do not track you across other companies' apps or websites.

3. Service Providers

  • Apple: In-App Purchase, AlarmKit and notifications.
  • RevenueCat: Subscription management.
  • Google Firebase: Analytics, crash reporting (Crashlytics), remote configuration, and storage of the sound files the App downloads.
  • Amplitude: Product analytics.
  • Microsoft Clarity: Interaction recordings and heatmaps, described above.
  • Supabase: The catalogue of sounds the App can download. The App only reads from it.
  • For legal reasons: We may disclose information if required by law or to protect our rights, safety, or property.

We do not sell, rent, or share your information with third parties for marketing purposes.

4. Data Security

Data the App sends to its service providers travels encrypted. Alarms, streaks and preferences are stored on your device. No system is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

5. Your Rights and Choices

  • Deleting the App removes everything it stored on your device.
  • Camera, microphone, notification and alarm permissions can be withdrawn at any time in your device settings, which may limit some features.
  • Manage or cancel your subscription in your App Store account settings.
  • To ask us to delete the analytics or purchase records held by our service providers for your installation, or to exercise other rights under laws such as the GDPR or CCPA, contact us at the address below.

6. Children's Privacy

The App is not intended for children under 13 years of age. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If we become aware of such collection, we will delete the information promptly.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically. We will notify you of significant changes via the App or other means. Continued use of the App after changes indicates your acceptance.
