Privacy Policy

Last updated: January 30, 2026

Alarmly ("we," "us," or "our") operates the Alarmly mobile application (the "App"). We are committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use the App. The App is available exclusively for iOS 26 users and utilizes Apple's AlarmKit for reliable alarm functionality.

1. Information We Collect

We may collect the following types of information:

  • Account Information: Automatically generated device-based account upon first use. No separate login or registration is required.
  • Usage Data: Alarm settings, wake-up challenge interactions (math, quiz, text, shake, memory, reaction), streak progress, and sleep sound preferences.
  • Device and Technical Information: Device ID, IP address, device type, OS version, app usage data, and crash reports for improving app stability.
  • Analytics Data: We may use third-party analytics services to collect anonymized usage data for improving the App.

We do not collect personal information such as names, email addresses, or payment details unless required for premium features through Apple's In-App Purchase system.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To provide core App features, including setting alarms via AlarmKit, playing relaxing sleep sounds, enforcing wake-up challenges, and tracking your wake-up streaks.
  • To personalize your experience with motivational messages and achievement tracking.
  • For app maintenance, troubleshooting, and performance improvements.
  • To communicate app updates or provide support if you contact us.
  • For internal analytics to understand usage patterns and enhance user experience.

3. Sharing Your Information

  • With third-party service providers: Limited to analytics, cloud storage for sounds, and subscription management (RevenueCat) under strict confidentiality agreements.
  • With Apple: For AlarmKit integration and In-App Purchase processing, as required by iOS.
  • For legal reasons: If required by law or to protect our rights, safety, or property.

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties for marketing purposes. All data sharing is limited to what's necessary for App functionality.

4. Data Security

We use industry-standard security measures, including encryption and access controls, to protect your data. Alarm data and preferences are stored locally on your device. However, no system is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

5. Your Rights and Choices

  • You can revoke alarm permissions at any time through your device settings, which may limit App features.
  • Access, correct, or delete your data by contacting us or uninstalling the App (which removes all locally stored data).
  • Manage your subscription through the App Store settings.
  • For users in jurisdictions with data protection laws (e.g., GDPR, CCPA), you may have additional rights such as data portability or objection to processing – contact us to exercise them.

6. Children's Privacy

The App is not intended for children under 13 years of age. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If we become aware of such collection, we will delete the information promptly.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically. We will notify you of significant changes via the App or other means. Continued use of the App after changes indicates your acceptance.
