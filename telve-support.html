Telve Support

Telve is an espresso and coffee tracking app for iPhone and iPad. Log your shots, build a bean library, dial in your grind, and get AI-powered brewing suggestions. If you need help, you're in the right place.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I log an espresso shot?

Open the Log tab, set your dose, yield, and time, pick the bean you're brewing, rate the shot, and save. Your stats and extraction history update automatically.

What is the AI Dial-In Coach?

When you tap the Coach, Telve looks at your recent shot, bean, and equipment, then suggests a single change to try next (for example, adjusting your grind). Suggestions are informational guidance, not guarantees.

Does my data sync across devices?

Yes. If you're signed in to iCloud, your beans, shots, and setups sync automatically across your iPhone and iPad through your private iCloud account. If you're not signed in to iCloud, Telve works fully offline on your device.

Will I lose my data if I reinstall the app?

If iCloud sync is enabled, your data is restored when you reinstall and sign in to the same Apple ID. Without iCloud, deleting the app removes its local data.

What does Telve Premium include?

Premium unlocks unlimited active beans and equipment setups, unlimited AI Coach usage, unlimited shareable shot cards, the Yearly Wrapped recap, and more. You can manage or cancel your subscription anytime in iOS Settings > your name > Subscriptions.

How do I restore a purchase?

Go to Settings inside the app and tap "Restore purchases" under the Subscription section.
